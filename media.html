Earlier

Central Saanich, 2008

Before crypto markets, Sean ran for Mayor of the District of Central Saanich, British Columbia, in the November 2008 municipal election. The race turned on housing supply, development approvals, and how much municipal red tape should shape the peninsula’s future. He ran a sharp, issues-first campaign — platform, local media including CFAX, and seanformayor.com — aimed at turning out the silent majority for growth with process discipline.

Earlier

Central Saanich, 2008

Before crypto markets, Sean ran a strong campaign for Mayor of the District of Central Saanich, British Columbia, in 2008. The platform centered on housing supply and support for projects such as Vantreight Hill, cutting municipal red tape, and speaking for what he called the “silent majority” against a vocal minority opposing development. He published a campaign site at seanformayor.com, sat for a CFAX Radio interview, and later wrote a memoir of the race, The Campaign for the Rational Mind.
